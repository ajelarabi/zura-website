Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Morocco Trade Group LLC ("ZURA," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through zura.beauty (the "Site"), how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Order & payment information: When you purchase through our Stripe checkout, Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address, and payment details directly. We do not store your card information.
  • Wholesale application information: When you submit the wholesale form, your name, clinic name, email, phone number, and message are sent to us via your email client.
  • Usage data: Basic technical data (such as browser type and pages visited) may be collected automatically to help us understand site performance.

How We Use Information

  • To fulfill and ship your order
  • To respond to wholesale inquiries
  • To communicate with you about your order or account
  • To improve our website and product offering

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers necessary to operate our business, such as Stripe (payment processing) and shipping carriers, and only to the extent needed to complete your order.

Data Retention

We retain order and communication records as long as necessary to fulfill orders, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing aj@zura.beauty.

Cookies

Our Site may use minimal cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
